Property opportunities in Asturias

Asturias combines coast and mountains under the Cantabrian Sea, with green valleys, wild beaches and centuries-old forests. Hiking in the Picos de Europa and surfing on the Costa Verde coexist in everyday life.

Oviedo and Gijón bring together culture and services; in Gijón Somió, Viesques and Cimavilla stand out, and in Oviedo, Montecerrao and Ciudad Naranco. Llanes, Ribadesella and Cudillero display historic centres and lively harbours.

Asturian pre-Romanesque architecture, with Santa María del Naranco and San Miguel de Lillo, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The A-8 runs along the coast and Asturias airport provides connections; cider, fabada and Cabrales add a gastronomic touch.
