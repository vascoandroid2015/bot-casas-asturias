      Asturias property market and price trends

      What is the average price of a property in Asturias?

      There are 241 properties for sale in Asturias on thinkSPAIN. The average price is € 191,130, with an average price per square metre for properties in Asturias of € 1,175.

      Which places in Asturias have the most properties for sale?

      The areas of Asturias with the most properties for sale are Pravia, Oviedo and Gijón.

      Where are the cheapest properties to buy in Asturias?

      Some of the cheapest areas in Asturias for properties are Grado, Mieres and Pravia.
